# Overview

## The problem

Small and mid-sized businesses outside of enterprise-software budgets run their books on a
mix of spreadsheets, paper, and disconnected point tools — one thing for invoicing, another
for expenses, nothing that ties inventory movement to the GL, no real audit trail. Founders
end up either doing it themselves badly, or paying an accountant to reconstruct records
after the fact instead of reviewing clean ones.

This product exists to give any business — in any country, any currency — one place where
every sale, purchase, expense, stock movement, and payroll run is captured once and flows
automatically into a real double-entry set of books. Not a replacement for an accountant:
it makes records organised and traceable enough for a professional to review, reconcile,
and file, without rebuilding the books from scratch first.

## Business model

Self-service SaaS: a business signs up, picks a plan, sets up their company profile
(country, base currency, industry), and starts using the product immediately. Optional
paid setup/onboarding assistance is offered as an upsell for businesses that want hands-on
help migrating from spreadsheets or another system.

## Target market

Small-to-mid businesses globally — not scoped to one country's tax authority or currency.
Deliberately the opposite of a Nigeria-specific tool: any business anywhere should be able
to sign up, pick their country and currency during setup, and get a system that fits their
jurisdiction's tax shape (VAT/GST/sales-tax-style, payroll-tax-style, withholding-style)
without the product assuming one specific country's rules.

## Guiding principle

**Enter it once, and it's everywhere it needs to be.** A sale becomes an invoice, becomes a
receivable if unpaid, becomes a stock movement if it ships something, becomes a GL posting,
becomes a number on every relevant report — automatically, from one entry point. The
product's job is to make that chain trustworthy enough that an accountant can pick up the
books and actually rely on them, not just look at them.

## Why this is a separate product from the founder's other tools

The founder has also built (and separately maintains) two Nigeria-specific finance tools —
one an internal single-tenant tool, one a separate multi-tenant SaaS play under a different
brand. Both are deliberately scoped to Nigerian tax rules (VAT/PAYE/WHT, NRS e-invoicing).
This product is the founder's own, personally-branded take on the same underlying problem,
built to work for any country from the start — no shared code, database, or brand with
either of those.
